Research · IEEE T-IV, submitted

Does the conformal guarantee travel?

6–25 ptscoverage lost when a 90% guarantee moves between driving datasets
The problem
Conformal prediction promises that a trajectory forecaster's uncertainty region contains the true path 90% of the time. That promise assumes test data looks like calibration data, which is rarely true when a car moves to a new city.
What I did
Measured coverage loss for Wayformer and AutoBot when moved between Argoverse 2, nuScenes and Waymo Open Motion. Proved that no label-free method can repair an unrestricted shift, then designed a scene-level Learn-then-Test rule and a betting test that restore the guarantee.
Outcome
The guarantee restored using only 150–200 labelled scenes, at about twice the oracle region size.

Research · IEEE T-IV, submitted

Certifying what a self-driving car does when it falls back

1,008 / 1,008checked pairs where lookup matched direct execution
The problem
When an autonomous vehicle detects danger it triggers a minimum-risk manoeuvre. Safety cases need evidence that the manoeuvre itself, not just the trigger, keeps harm below a target.
What I did
Showed that a closed-loop rollout depends only on the tick at which the fallback fires, so every trigger, threshold and latency can be evaluated by lookup. Then certified the executed manoeuvre with risk-ordered fixed-sequence testing in ScenarioNet/MetaDrive with reactive traffic.
Outcome
5.4% realised harm against a 7.5% target, replicated across three predictors, two traffic models and six held-out cities.

04Research

Publications

Three first-author manuscripts submitted to IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles in 2026, with co-authors R. Z. Saleem and H. Maryam. Every experiment is pre-registered and released with per-scenario records.

01

Does the Conformal Guarantee Travel? Measuring, Auditing and Repairing Coverage Loss in Cross-Dataset Trajectory Prediction

H. W. Saleem, R. Z. Saleem, H. Maryam · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles, 2026 · Code ↗
Summary

Shows that 90% conformal regions for trajectory forecasters lose 6–25 percentage points of coverage across Argoverse 2, nuScenes and Waymo Open Motion; proves label-free repair is impossible under unrestricted shift; restores the guarantee with a scene-level Learn-then-Test rule and a betting test from 150–200 labelled scenes.

Under review
02

Certifying the Outcome of Autonomous Vehicle Fallback Triggers: Closed-Loop Evaluation Under Latency and Dataset Shift

H. W. Saleem, R. Z. Saleem, H. Maryam · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles, 2026 · Code ↗
Summary

Evaluates every trigger, threshold and latency by lookup (matching direct execution in all 1,008 checked pairs) and certifies the executed minimum-risk manoeuvre with risk-ordered fixed-sequence testing: 5.4% realised harm against a 7.5% target across three predictors, two traffic models and six held-out cities.

Under review
03

When Does Per-Object Arbitration Help? Late Fusion over Frozen Camera, LiDAR, and Radar Detectors in Simulated and Real Adverse Weather

H. W. Saleem, R. Z. Saleem, H. Maryam · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Vehicles, 2026 · Code ↗
Summary

An ~18k-parameter per-object router beats per-frame fusion by 0.077 mAP in simulated weather and 0.064 mAP in real rain, snow, fog and darkness (CADC, Boreas, K-Radar), and maps where the gain disappears: without radar, sim-to-real, and with fine-tuned LiDAR.
